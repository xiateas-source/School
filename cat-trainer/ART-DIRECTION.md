# Cat Trainer art direction

The uploaded family and pet printable illustrations are the canonical visual language.

## Required

- Warm white and pastel paper-world surfaces
- Raster WebP/PNG character and object art as the dominant visuals
- Exact family cast: Mom, Abba, Sirus, Arlo, and Wesley
- Rounded playful typography, dashed cutout edges, and soft shadows
- Illustrated cats, beds, pillows, and café objects

## Avoid

- Dark glowing dashboard interfaces
- Procedural SVG cats or characters
- Generic AI-game gradients
- Mismatched clip art
- Replacing the established family appearances

SVG may remain for a small app icon or minor utility mark, but it must not dominate the child-facing experience.
